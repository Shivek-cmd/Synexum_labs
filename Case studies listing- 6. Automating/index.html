The Context

High lead volume, low follow-through

A medium-sized plumbing business serving a local metro area was generating a steady flow of leads from its website, Google Ads, emergency service requests, and phone inquiries.

Demand was strong, but bookings were inconsistent. The issue wasn’t marketing - it was slow and unreliable lead follow-up that caused potential customers to move on to competitors.

This case study outlines how automating lead follow-ups transformed response times, increased conversions, and unlocked revenue growth without additional marketing spend.

The Challenge

Manual follow-up was costing revenue

Before automation, a small office team manually handled every lead. This approach created operational strain and lost opportunities.

  • Leads were contacted hours later or the next day
  • After-hours and busy-period leads were missed entirely
  • Inconsistent messaging and no structured follow-up
  • Leads frequently chose competitors who responded faster

In emergency plumbing situations, speed determines trust. Delayed responses directly translated into lost jobs.

Our Approach

Automated, fast, and human-centered follow-up

1
Centralized Lead Capture Weeks 1

All leads from website forms, ads, emergency requests, and calls were routed into a single CRM. This ensured visibility, ownership, and zero lead loss.

2
Immediate Automated Responses Weeks 2

Within 60 seconds of sending in a form or query, prospects got the following:

  • A personalized SMS message letting them know they got their request
  • An email with information about the service and what to do next
  • This rapid answer made clients feel like help was on the way.
3
Smart Follow-Up Sequences Weeks 3

If a lead didn’t book immediately, the system triggered timed follow-ups at two hours, one day, and three days - maintaining momentum without being intrusive.

4
Scheduling & Team Alerts Weeks 4

Leads could book appointments instantly via scheduling links, while office staff received real-time alerts for replies, bookings, and high-intent keywords.
